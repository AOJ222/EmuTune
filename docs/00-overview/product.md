# Product

EmuTune is a premium, evidence-driven emulator optimisation platform for Android gaming handhelds first (AYN, Odin, Retroid and similar landscape devices), and phones/tablets second.

Its purpose: determine the best verified way to run a particular game on the user's exact hardware, explain why, automatically apply it when technically safe, verify that the result is genuinely better, and roll back safely if it is not.

## What it is not

- Not another compatibility wiki.
- Not an AI chat app.
- Not merely an emulator launcher.
- Not a database of community settings.
- Not a collection of conflicting recommendations the user must interpret.

The application **makes a decision**. The reasoning and evidence remain inspectable underneath, but are not forced on ordinary users.

## Core principle

> Recommendations are hypotheses until measurements verify them.

AI, community reports, changelogs and historical results may propose candidates. They do not establish truth. Measured, correctly contextualised evidence establishes truth.

## Second principle

> This product is curated, not accumulated.

Every concept has one canonical definition; every item of information has one authoritative home; every emulator integration lives behind an adapter; every status comes from central domain logic.
